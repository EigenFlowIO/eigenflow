## Drafting objective

Frame the higher-order experiment formally: repeated controlled measurements, Q(l,p,t), structural displacement, and the distinction between structural evidence and semantic/causal interpretation.

## Proposed content

The central theoretical move is `Q(l,p) -> Q(l,p,t)` while preserving the population-relational interpretation. The comparison object is a controlled repeated experiment, not a generic embedding-distance comparison. Structural displacement is descriptive evidence conditional on a measurement model; it cannot by itself establish learning, forgetting, causality, or feature localization.
