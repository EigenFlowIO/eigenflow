## Scientific disagreement

Draft move: define the conceptual object and qualify the inferential claim before presenting implementation details. Core proposition to preserve or sharpen: Interpretability research often involves contested definitions and incomplete evidence. Disagreement is expected. When disputing a claim, prefer: - a counterexample; - a failing test; - a reproducible experiment; - a clearer definition; - a competing interpretation tied to the same observations; - evidence that a conclusion depends on an unreported measurement choice. Do not treat methodological disagreement as evidence of bad faith without independent grounds.
