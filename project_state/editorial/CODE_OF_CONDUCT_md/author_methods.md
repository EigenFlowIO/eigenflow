## Our standards

Instructional content to preserve: Eigenflow welcomes technical and scientific collaboration conducted with professional respect. Expected behavior includes: - discussing evidence, code, methods, and claims rather than attacking people; - making disagreements reproducible where possible; - distinguishing implementation defects from scientific disagreements; - acknowledging uncertainty and correcting errors; - respecting privacy and confidential information; - giving contributors appropriate credit.

## Scientific disagreement

Draft move: connect this section to a practitioner decision, prerequisite, or transfer task. Use a concrete research choice or worked contrast. Instructional content to preserve: Interpretability research often involves contested definitions and incomplete evidence. Disagreement is expected. When disputing a claim, prefer: - a counterexample; - a failing test; - a reproducible experiment; - a clearer definition; - a competing interpretation tied to the same observations; - evidence that a conclusion depends on an unreported measurement choice. Do not treat methodological disagreement as evidence of bad faith without independent grounds.
